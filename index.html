Chapter 04 • From the heart

Mera Message ❤️

Happy Birthday, Begumm ji ❤️🎂✨

Begumm ji… Aaj ka din mere liye sirf ek normal date nahi hai. Aaj ka din woh din hai jis din duniya ko aap mili thi… aur shayad meri zindagi ko bhi ek aisi wajah milni thi jiske liye dil thoda aur feel karna seekhe. ❤️

I genuinely hope ki aapki life ka ye naya chapter aapke liye utni hi khoobsurat khushiyan lekar aaye, jitni khoobsurti aap apne aas-paas ke logon ki life mein laati hain.

Kabhi kabhi kuch log bas “log” banke nahi aate… woh dheere-dheere aadat ban jaate hain, phir sukoon, phir smile ka reason. Aap mere liye kuch aisi hi hain.

I don't know ki main words mein exactly explain kar paunga ya nahi ki aap mere liye kya hain… because kuch feelings ko explain nahi kiya ja sakta, bas feel kiya ja sakta hai. Aur aap unhi feelings mein se ek hain. ❤️

Aaj main bas aapke liye achhi cheezein wish karna chahta hoon. I wish aapki smile hamesha genuine rahe, aapke dreams slowly beautifully reality ban jaayein, aur aapko kabhi khud ko alone feel na karna pade.

Aapki feelings important hain. Aapke dreams important hain. Aapki happiness important hai. Aur aap khud bhi bahut important hain.

“Na chaand ki khwahish hai, na sitaaron ki farmaish hai, bas aap hamesha muskuraati rahein, mere liye itni si guzarish hai.” ❤️

Life perfect nahi hoti. Kabhi good days, kabhi bad days. But difficult days aapki entire story nahi hain. Your story is still being written, aur mujhe hope hai ki aage ke pages bahut beautiful honge. ✨

Don't just chase success. Chase peace too. Don't just chase achievements. Chase moments that make you genuinely happy. Laugh a lot, dream big, make memories, aur dil khol kar jeena. ❤️

May Allah bless you with happiness, peace, success and countless beautiful moments. May every genuine dua you make find its way to you. May your future be brighter than your past. 🤲🏻❤️

Aaj se aapki life ka ek aur chapter start ho raha hai. New dreams. New memories. New reasons to smile. And most importantly… full of happiness.

Happy Birthday once again, meri special si Begumm ji. ❤️🎂✨

Stay happy. Stay blessed. Stay smiling. And please… never forget how special you are. 🫶🏻
